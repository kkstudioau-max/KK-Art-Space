At What Cost?

Mixed media sculptural vessel | Plaster, handmade joomchi paper, acrylic paint | 270 mm diameter | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $180 AUD
Enquire about this work

At What Cost? explores the unseen price we sometimes pay simply to survive, belong, or become what is expected of us.

Its fractured, irregular edges and delicate layers speak to the ways life can wear us down, leaving marks that are not always visible to others. Beneath the torn surface lies a sense of vulnerability, revealing the fragility that often exists alongside strength.

The piece invites us to consider the sacrifices we make, the parts of ourselves we lose along the way, and the question of whether the cost is always worth what we gain.
