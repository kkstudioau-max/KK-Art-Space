Weathered Memories

Mixed-media artwork on board | 650 × 580 mm | Framed | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $395 AUD
Enquire about this work See it in your space

Built through layers of plaster, fabric, paint, charcoal and found impressions, this mixed-media work explores the traces left behind through change, damage and repair.

Muted charcoal and smoky blue tones shift across an eroded surface, interrupted by exposed fibres, rust-stained marks and fragments that appear partially buried or worn away. A stark white vertical mark cuts through the quieter right-hand side — deliberately unresolved, allowing the viewer to decide what it might represent.

The work rewards closer inspection. Details emerge and disappear depending on distance and light, encouraging the eye to wander rather than settle on a single focal point. What initially appears chaotic begins to reveal connections between marks, materials and remnants.

There is no prescribed interpretation. Like memory and experience, the surface can be read differently by every viewer — and that ambiguity is an intentional part of the work.
