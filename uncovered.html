Uncovered

Mixed media on board | 650 × 650 mm | Framed | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $495 AUD
Enquire about this work

Uncovered explores what lies beneath the surface — the traces, histories and experiences that remain even when they are no longer immediately visible.

Dense cellular forms emerge from a weathered landscape of layered greys, softened blues and fractured marks, gradually dissolving into quieter space. From a distance, the work shifts between complexity and stillness; up close, subtle impressions, imperfections and remnants reveal themselves.

Built through layers of mixed media, abrasion and pigment, Uncovered reflects the way time can obscure, alter and expose what has come before. Rather than concealing its irregularities, the work allows them to remain visible — evidence of what has been carried, changed and endured.
