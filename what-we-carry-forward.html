What We Carry Forward

Mixed media on board | 1085 × 900 mm | Framed | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $2200 AUD
Enquire about this work See it in your space

What We Carry Forward considers the way memories are carried, reshaped and passed between generations.

Faded pattern, softened colour and worn surfaces suggest fragments of lives already lived — details recalled imperfectly, stories revisited, and moments that remain long after their original setting has disappeared. Pink gives way to blue before gradually moving into a quieter, lighter space, reflecting the way memories can move beyond the person who first experienced them.

Some memories are our own. Others are given to us through stories, repeated so often that they become part of our own understanding of family and belonging.

Rather than representing memory as something that simply fades, the work considers what happens when it is shared — how the people we have loved can continue forward through the stories we tell, and eventually through generations who never knew them themselves.

For me, the family table is one of the places where that continuation happens — through conversation, recollection, humour and repeated stories, those who are absent are given a place again, and their memories are carried forward by the people sitting there now.
