On Quiet Nights

Mixed media on canvas | 1050 × 780 mm | Framed | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $1450 AUD
Enquire about this work See it in your space

Grief does not always fade with time. Some losses leave a sorrow that becomes part of who we are, quietly remaining beneath the surface of everyday life.

On Quiet Nights explores the coexistence of grief and joy. A dark, unwavering band cuts through a weathered landscape of muted, earthy tones, representing a sorrow that never entirely leaves us. Yet light remains, both above and below, reflecting the happiness, connection and meaning that continue alongside loss.

It is in life’s quieter moments, when the distractions fall away, that we sometimes find ourselves returning to that darkness.

Not because we have failed to move forward, but because some people are simply never forgotten.
