Enough?

Mixed media on glass bottle, plaster, handwritten paper, wax and aged finishes | 230 mm (H) × 100 mm (W) × 40 mm (D) | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $95 AUD
Enquire about this work

Enough? explores the lasting impact of the words we hear, the expectations we carry, and the quiet struggle to believe we are enough.

An aged glass bottle, partially concealed beneath eroded, heavily textured surfaces, holds a handwritten message. Its words are fragmented and obscured, inviting the viewer to look closer, shift their perspective and piece together what remains.

The contrast between the fragile glass and its weathered exterior reflects the way our sense of self can become buried beneath years of criticism, judgement and self-doubt.

Yet the message remains, waiting to be discovered.

Enough? asks us to consider how deeply these experiences shape the way we see ourselves — and whether we can learn to recognise our own worth beneath them.
