Ash Relic

Mixed media sculptural vessel | 370 mm diameter × 30 mm high | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $180 AUD
Enquire about this work

Ash Relic explores what remains after something has been altered, worn away or endured.

Its shallow, plate-like form carries an aged and weathered surface, with irregular edges and layers that feel almost excavated rather than made. Soft, ash-toned finishes sit against darker traces and raw textural details, creating the sense of an object that has survived its own history.

Part of the Ash Collection, Ash Relic reflects the tension between fragility and resilience — the beauty found in erosion, imperfection and what remains.
