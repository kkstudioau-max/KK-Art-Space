Frankie

BOS-012 · The Boston Collection · Individual sculpture

Enquire about this sculpture

Meet Frankie — a rather unconventional little character with a personality all his own. Crafted from distressed textiles, with visible stitching, frayed edges and carefully considered imperfections, Frankie wears his repairs with pride. A weathered leather collar and handmade KK star tag complete this one-of-a-kind sculptural companion.
