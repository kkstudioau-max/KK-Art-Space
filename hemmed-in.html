Hemmed In

1050 × 780 mm | Black frame
AVAILABLE | $1200 AUD
Enquire about this work

There are times in life when the circumstances surrounding us begin to feel like walls. Shaped by external pressures, relationships, expectations and our own experiences, these boundaries can gradually restrict our sense of freedom and possibility.

Yet confinement is rarely straightforward. Even circumstances that cause distress can offer a sense of familiarity, safety and predictability. What holds us back can also be what we are most reluctant to leave behind.

Through layered textures, weathered surfaces and a restrained palette of storm blues and charcoal, Hemmed In explores this tension between restriction and security.

The central space is enclosed, yet its boundaries are neither complete nor impenetrable. Areas of openness suggest the possibility of change, while acknowledging that recognising a way forward and being able to take it are not always the same.

Hemmed In is a reflection on the complexities of feeling trapped, the comfort we sometimes find in familiarity, and the possibility of moving beyond the boundaries that contain us.
