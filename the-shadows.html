The Shadows

1016 × 762 mm (40 × 30 inches) | Black frame
AVAILABLE | $1200 AUD
Enquire about this work See it in your space

Our perception of the world is shaped not only by what exists around us, but by what we carry within.

From childhood, we develop beliefs and expectations that influence how we interpret our experiences, the intentions of others, and the world around us. Often, without conscious awareness, we seek meaning that reinforces what we already believe.

The Shadows explores the fragile boundary between perception and reality. The three ambiguous figures resist a single interpretation. Are they threatening or protective? Are they approaching, retreating, or simply observing?

What one person perceives as something to fear, another may experience as a source of comfort.

Perhaps the shadows themselves are neither.

Perhaps it is what we bring to them that gives them meaning.
