The Boston Collection

Sculptural objects | Weathered forms | Curated arrangements

An exploration of organic forms, tactile surfaces and the understated beauty of objects that appear to have lived another life.

Individually handcrafted using mixed media techniques, each piece embraces the irregularities, subtle variations and distinctive character of its making.

Discover individual sculptures, thoughtfully curated pairings and inspiration for creating your own arrangements.

Customers may purchase individual sculptures, select a curated pairing or get in touch to enquire about creating their own combination.

The Art of Arrangement

Explore the possibilities of the Boston Collection through a series of thoughtfully styled sculptural arrangements.

Combining contrasting forms, weathered surfaces and complementary tones, each composition demonstrates how individual objects can come together to create something distinctive.

Some arrangements feature pieces retained in the artist's personal collection and are presented for inspiration rather than purchase.

Featuring BOS-001 · Original Plate, BOS-002 · Original Large Bowl, BOS-003 · Original Small Bowl and BOS-004 · Original Sphere Trio. These originals are part of the artist's personal collection and are not available for purchase.
