The Grace We Give Ourselves

Mixed media on board | 1260 × 630 mm | Framed | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $995 AUD
Enquire about this work See it in your space

Some of the hardest second chances are the ones we must learn to give ourselves.

A fractured formation moves across a weathered surface, its layers revealing traces of what has come before. Fine cracks give way to deeper ruptures, while softened edges and muted tones suggest the quiet passage of time.

This work reflects on our capacity to extend compassion and forgiveness to others, yet struggle to afford ourselves that same kindness.

The fractures remain. They are not concealed or erased, but become part of something changed. A reminder that beginning again does not require us to undo our past, nor does it demand perfection.

Sometimes, the greatest act of grace is simply allowing ourselves another chance.
