Can You Really See Me?

Mixed media sculptural relief on board | 1000 × 1000 × 170 mm | Framed | Recessed lighting | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $3200 AUD
Enquire about this work See it in your space

Can You Really See Me? explores the space between what we reveal to the world and what we keep hidden beneath the surface.

Layers of folded fabric create a sense of protection and concealment, while fractured, weathered surfaces suggest the marks left by life’s experiences. Subtle variations in texture, shadow and light reveal details that might otherwise go unnoticed.

The contrast between fragile, damaged edges and strong, flowing forms reflects the complex relationship between vulnerability and resilience.

This work invites the viewer to look beyond outward appearances, to question their first impressions and consider the experiences we cannot always see in others.

Sometimes, the most important parts of a person are the ones that remain hidden.
