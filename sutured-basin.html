Sutured Basin

Mixed media, plaster, fibre and wire | 80 mm diameter × 50 mm high | Original 1/1
AVAILABLE | $395 AUD
Enquire about this work

Sutured Basin explores the lasting physical and emotional traces of self-harm.

Its fractured form has been repeatedly pierced and sutured, holding the vessel together while leaving areas of loss exposed. Hollows, scars and irregularities remain across the surface — evidence that repair does not always mean restoration to what existed before.

The work reflects on the aftermath of injury: the point at which wounds may have closed, yet their history remains written into the body. Rather than attempting to conceal its damage, Sutured Basin carries it openly. Its stitches hold the fragmented form together, while the missing sections acknowledge that some experiences leave permanent changes.

Repaired, altered, and still whole.
