Ash Reliquary

Mixed media, plaster compounds, found elements and acrylic finishes | 160 mm diameter × 200 mm high | Original 1/1
NOT FOR SALE
Enquire about this work

Ash Reliquary evokes the feeling of an object unearthed and carried through time.

Its irregular form and heavily worked surface are marked by hollows, impressions and exposed circular details that emerge through layers of muted ash, stone and charcoal tones. Rather than concealing its imperfections, the piece allows them to become part of its history — traces of erosion, damage and survival held within the surface.

Hand-formed and impossible to replicate, Ash Reliquary exists as a true 1/1 original. Like a traditional reliquary — a vessel made to hold something of significance — it speaks to what we preserve, what remains, and the marks left behind.
