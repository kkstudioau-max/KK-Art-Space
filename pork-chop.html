Pork Chop

The Wayward Collection

Original, one-of-a-kind mixed-media sculpture | Approximately 400 mm (L) × 290 mm (H) × 270 mm (W) | Papier-mâché, cowhide, fabric, hessian, plaster and natural timber
| $695 AUD
Enquire about this work

Meet Pork Chop. A rather unconventional little character with an oversized snout, curious eyes and a personality all his own.

With his rounded body, sturdy timber legs and wonderfully unruly tail, Pork Chop is an endearing celebration of individuality and imperfection. His slightly bewildered expression and unusual proportions give him a quiet charm, while his weathered surfaces reveal a more considered, tactile quality.

Handcrafted using papier-mâché, cowhide, fabric, hessian and timber, Pork Chop brings together contrasting materials and textures. Aged finishes, natural markings and subtle variations in his surface give him the appearance of a character who has already lived a rather interesting life.

Part of the Wayward Collection, Pork Chop embraces the unexpected, finding beauty and humour in the wonderfully imperfect.

A little rough around the edges, but entirely comfortable in his own skin.

Important Safety Information

Pork Chop is a sculptural artwork intended for decorative display only and is not a toy. Keep out of reach of young children.
