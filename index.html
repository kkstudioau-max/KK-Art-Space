A little about me

Welcome to my space.

I’m a mixed-media artist based in Newcastle, Australia, creating tactile works that explore the tension between fragility and resilience. I’m drawn to texture, worn surfaces, cracks, layers and imperfections — the marks that suggest a history rather than something untouched. Using materials such as plaster, fabric, timber, paper, sand and paint, I build each piece intuitively, allowing the work to evolve as I go. My art is often inspired by the things we carry beneath the surface: memory, trauma, healing, connection and the quieter parts of the human experience.
