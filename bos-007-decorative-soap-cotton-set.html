Decorative Soap & Cotton Set

BOS-007 · The Boston Collection · Individual sculpture

Enquire about this sculpture

A collection of handcrafted imitation soaps with softly weathered surfaces, accompanied by textured cotton fabric. Subtle variations in form, colour and finish give each piece the appearance of an object worn by time. Designed as sculptural accessories, they can be displayed individually or incorporated into the Boston Collection's washroom arrangements.
