Sculptural Jug

BOS-005 · The Boston Collection · Individual sculpture

Enquire about this sculpture

A handcrafted sculptural jug with a softly weathered surface, irregular contours and a worn timber handle. Its restrained palette and aged appearance give the piece the character of an object shaped by time and use. Designed as a decorative sculpture, it can stand alone or form part of a curated arrangement.

Artwork details

  • Dimensions240 mm high × 150 mm wide (including handle)
  • MaterialsMixed media, papier-mâché, hand-applied textured finish and timber handle
  • EditionOne of a kind
  • Price$295 AUD

Decorative sculpture only; not intended to hold liquids.
