Warts and All

Mixed media on board | 920 × 610 mm | Framed
AVAILABLE | $695 AUD
Enquire about this work

Warts and All explores the pressure to conceal what is imperfect, damaged or uncomfortable. Society encourages us to soften the evidence of ageing, hide our scars and disguise the emotional fractures we carry.

In this work, the cracked surface is not repaired or covered. It becomes the focal point — exposed, imperfect and deliberately beautiful.

Layers of texture, fractured plaster and smoky blue staining create a surface that feels weathered and vulnerable, while the surrounding pale field gives the broken areas space to breathe.

Warts and All is a quiet rejection of the need to appear untouched. It honours the marks we carry and the beauty that can exist when we stop trying to hide them.
